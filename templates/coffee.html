About Big Bear Coffee Roasting Company

At Big Bear Coffee Roasting Company, they believe that a cup of coffee is beautiful in its simplicity, but the process from seed to cup is far from simple. It's an unbelievable result stemming from growing, farming, processing & sorting the fruit.

Artisan Roasting

Using a Diedrich Roaster, they constantly evaluate and experiment with each new arrival, searching for that bean's optimal finish temperature and roast time.

No Automation

Their roaster is not automated and they don't use profiling software. Every batch is carefully crafted by skilled roasters who understand the craft.

Health Benefits

Their cold brew process takes 72 hours, yielding lower acidity and providing antioxidants, phytochemicals, and other nutrients without the discomfort of hot coffee.

Local Craft

Located at 41080 Big Bear Blvd, they're a true local business that understands the mountain community and the importance of quality coffee.
