You Built Something That Worked.
Now You Want Out.

You don't want to burn it down. But something has to change.

If you're a solopreneur who's successful on paper but waking up thinking, 'I don't want to do this shit anymore,' you're not alone. You've hit the builder's paradox—where the thing you built to set you free has become the thing that's boxing you in.

The Builder's Paradox

You are a builder. You build because you have always craved basking in the tranquility of craftsmanship. You build because your soul will never let it go. You build because you love quality and intention. You carved your own path to get your work to your people, but now it feels like you are living within the machine—the same machinery that you spent your life breaking free from.

What you crave is to re-imagine a system with soul. A way to feel good about bringing more of what you build to more of who you build for. You feel unsettled, but it is not clear why. You feel grateful with abundance, but not vibrant.

Island A: Where You Are Now

You're trapped between only two options: keep getting ground down by the system that you created for yourself, or burn it all down. You're angry because you thought that building systems to bring your own creative work products directly to the marketplace would give you lifelong joy and freedom, but you do not feel those things.

  • Feeling trapped by your own success
  • Waking up dreading the work you once loved
  • Stuck between grinding it out or burning it down
  • Successful on paper but empty inside
  • Living within the machine you built to escape

Island B: Where You Want to Be

Free to explore an exciting array of options. No longer trapped between two possibilities. You look elated, vibrant, alert, curious, hopeful and tranquil because you no longer are trapped within the system you built, but rather the system you built is supporting your enthusiasm to build.

  • Reconnected with the soul of your work
  • Excited about new possibilities and directions
  • Systems that support rather than constrain you
  • Feeling both accomplished and creatively fulfilled
  • Thriving with strong spine and soft heart
