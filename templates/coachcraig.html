Your Guide Through the Builder's Paradox

I'm Craig—a builder, coach, and your host at Still Waters Retreats. I help successful entrepreneurs and solopreneurs who feel trapped by the very systems they created to set themselves free.

If you're waking up thinking, "I don't want to do this anymore," even though you're successful on paper—you're not broken. You've hit the builder's paradox, and I can help you find your way through.

The Builder's Paradox

You built something remarkable. You carved your own path, created systems to bring your work directly to your people, and achieved what most only dream of. But somewhere along the way, the machinery you built to escape the 9-to-5 grind became its own kind of trap.

You're not looking to burn it all down. You're looking for a third option—one where the system you built supports your enthusiasm to build, rather than boxing you in.

That's where I come in.

How I Can Help

I draw from the deepest waters of effective psychology and artistic process to help creative entrepreneurs navigate the builder's paradox. My approach combines business strategy with creative soul-work:

  • Campfires & Vampires: Create an energy map to identify what drains you and what lights you up, so you can reclaim the soul of your work
  • Soulful Systems: Build approaches that preserve your creative essence while embracing the efficiencies that drive growth
  • Drifting & Driving: Find the balance between letting work flow naturally and taking intentional action
  • Flow & Frustration: Navigate the natural cycles of creative work and turn frustration into breakthrough
  • The Fire Starter: Expand your options so you can break free from feeling trapped between only grinding it out or burning it down

I've walked this path myself. I understand what it's like to build something successful only to feel constrained by it. More importantly, I've found a way through—and I can help you find yours.

Coaching & Hospitality

At Still Waters Retreats, I've created spaces specifically designed for the kind of deep work and reflection that breaks the builder's paradox. Fast Wi-Fi when you need it. Mountain stillness when you want it. And a host who understands the rhythm of focused work and creative restoration.

Whether you book a cabin for solo deep work, join a guided retreat, or work with me one-on-one, you'll find an environment that supports both your business success and your creative soul.
