About This Cabin

Escape to our mountain cottage in the coveted lower moonridge neighborhood that is a 5 minute walk to the free Big Bear Trolley (Red Line) to Bear Mountain or Snow Summit, running every 30 mins. We provide crisp fresh white towels, a rare luxury in AirBnBs! Revel in the large, secluded deck with fire pit & BBQ. A winter wonderland awaits you with a toasty wood burning fireplace, modern kitchen, and soft comfortable beds to make this the perfect retreat.

Victoria Pines in Big Bear has soaring vaulted ceilings in the bedrooms and large windows throughout the cottage that bring light through the pines into every room. A 56" Phillips QLED Roku TV in the downstairs living room allows you to log in to all your streaming services like Netflix, Prime, YouTube TV, etc.! The upstairs king bedroom also has a Roku TV. We run both in guest mode, so you can rest assured that all your accounts will automatically be signed out on your checkout date. We also have lightning fast internet!
