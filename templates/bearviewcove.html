About This Cabin

Mountain peace and quiet awaits you in this cozy, clean and bright perfect mountain cabin in the coveted upper moonridge area. The cabin has one queen bedroom, one bathroom and an open loft with two full beds. We furnish with crisp fresh white towels, a rare luxury in AirBnBs! Grill, Ski, Hike, Relax and enjoy everything the mountains have to offer. A modern kitchen and a wonderful deck with a treetop view will be the perfect backdrop for family memories enjoying Big Bear.

Bear View Cove Cabin in Big Bear has a soaring cathedral window that brings light into the living room and loft. We include Roku in guest mode on both upstairs and downstairs TVs so you can bring your streaming accounts like Netflix, Prime, Apple TV, etc. with you and rest assured that they will automatically be signed out on the date you choose. We also have lightning fast internet. All beds have 5 star hotel quality bedding and downstairs bedroom has two large windows letting in lots of light. Brass bedside lamps with usb chargers make charging devices a breeze.
